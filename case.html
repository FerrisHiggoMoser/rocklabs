CORNE CHOC PRO / NEOPRENE TRAVEL CASE

Closes like

One piece of neoprene sewn into a tube, with one seam across the middle that splits it into two pockets. That seam is also the hinge: fold the case shut, keycaps facing in, and one loop of black 30 mm elastic holds it closed. A small pocket on the back holds the cables. Two main seams, all by hand. Just the keyboard: no puck, no attachment.

03 / HOW TO MAKE IT

Two main seams, by hand.

You need

  • 3 mm neoprene fabric with fabric on both sides, at least 349 × 300 mm
  • Strong thread and thick, sharp hand-sewing needles; a cheap leather sewing kit has both, plus a thimble and an awl for poking the holes first
  • 8–12 sewing clips or clothes pegs
  • 30 mm black elastic, about 35 cm
  • Ruler or tape measure, tailor's chalk and fabric scissors
  • A 136 × 96 mm piece of thin stiff plastic or card for the divider

The one stitch you need

Every seam is a backstitch. Knot the thread, bring the needle up through the fabric, then push it back down about 5 mm behind where it came out. Bring it up again 5 mm in front of the thread and repeat. Each stitch meets the last one, so the line is solid. Knot it again at the end.

Steps

  1. Mark a 349 × 300 mm rectangle on the neoprene with chalk and cut it out. From the leftover, cut a 120 × 85 mm patch for the cable pocket.
  2. Lay the patch on the outside face of the big piece, 22 mm from the fold line and 19 mm from one short end, as in the diagram. Backstitch it on along both sides and the edge nearest the middle, 5 mm in. Leave the edge nearest the end open.
  3. Fold the big piece in half so the two 300 mm sides meet, outside faces together, so the patch ends up inside. Clip them every 5 cm.
  4. Backstitch those two sides together, 10 mm in from the edge. You now have a tube, open at both ends.
  5. Turn the tube right side out, like a sock, and flatten it with the seam along one side.
  6. Slide a half in from each end, keycaps up and the cable pocket underneath, to check the fit. Then take them out again.
  7. Measure 150 mm from one end and chalk a line straight across. Backstitch along it through both layers. That one seam makes the two pockets and is the hinge.
  8. For the strap, cut 313 mm of elastic, overlap the ends by 20 mm and sew a small square through both layers to make a loop.
  9. To pack, unplug the cables, coil them and push them into the cable pocket. Slide a half into each pocket, keycaps up and the cable pocket underneath. Lay the divider on one half, fold the case at the middle seam so the keycaps face each other, and slip the strap over it. The strap also holds both openings and the cable pocket shut.
  10. The first time it's packed, put 4–5 stitches through the strap and the middle seam where they cross, so the strap stays on the case. To open it, roll the strap off the top half; it stays attached at the spine.

Don't pack it damp. It protects against bumps and rubbing in a bag, not crushing.
