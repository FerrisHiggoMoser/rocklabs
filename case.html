03 / PATTERN

Print it at actual size.

  1. Print the pattern on A4 at 100 % and check that the bar measures 50 mm. Trace it onto the neoprene twice and cut.
  2. Fold each piece at the fold line, outside faces together, and hold the sides with sewing clips.
  3. Sew both long sides 10 mm in. Leave the short ends open: together they are the opening. Neoprene does not fray, so they need no hem.
  4. Turn each sleeve right side out and slide a half in. Sew each elastic loop shut with its overlap and slip both loops over the packed sleeves.
