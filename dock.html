01 / THE CONNECTION

Set down. Snap on.

The straight edge sits beside the keyboard with a 0.3 mm assembly gap. A slim tongue slides under the case and meets four adhesive steel targets. The circular cradle supports the puck on a ledge.

The open underside clears the puck’s existing feet. A rear notch leaves its USB port accessible, and four holes keep the case screws reachable. Lift the puck out whenever you need it separately.

02 / THE FIT

Measure once. Print the small one first.

Keebart’s own product image shows an 8.2 mm case profile. Exact case contours, foot locations, and keycap height are not confirmed for your keyboard.

This default uses a 2 mm keyboard-foot height, a 16 mm underside-to-keycap height, and the V6 CAD’s 4.5 mm puck feet. Check those measurements, plus 36 mm of straight case edge, with the fit coupon. Matching the case rim instead requires keyboard risers.

STEP · millimetres

Hardware: four Ø8 × 1 mm magnets, four Ø8 × 0.2 mm ferromagnetic steel targets, approximately 0.1 mm adhesive behind each target, and glue for the magnet pockets. Plastic and aluminium need the added steel targets. This is not a MagSafe ring mount.
