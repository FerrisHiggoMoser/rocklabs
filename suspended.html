01 / BUILT TO COME WITH YOU

Clamp the case. Capture the puck.

Two screws draw padded jaws around the keyboard’s case edge. Reinforced arms carry the angled cradle; a removable ring holds the puck above its supporting shelf. The keyboard’s own suspension mount carries the combined assembly.

The puck stays in its original enclosure, with its feet attached and USB accessible at the rear. Remove the three ring screws to take it out. The attachment extends below the keyboard’s usual feet and is intended for an elevated setup.

02 / FIT YOUR SETUP

A little measuring before the lift.

The default needs an 8.2 mm thick closed case, 56 mm of straight edge, a bare 4 mm top ledge, and 14 mm of clear underside. Print the fit check first and test full key travel, ports, feet, and suspension-mount clearance.

These are configurable fit assumptions. If that ledge or underside space is missing, the default clamp does not fit. Test grip and normal touch pressure close above a soft surface before suspended use; printed strength and long-term holding force are not yet measured.
