# Minimum enclosure — four-screw version

Body: **47 × 50 × 13.7 mm**, excluding rubber feet. This retains the minimum enclosure size while using all four M2 corner screws and four M2×4 heat-set inserts.

The compact bosses occupy the otherwise unused corner regions outside the Ø40.5 mm circular sensor pocket. The FFC and four AWG30 conductors share the straight 8 mm center channel. The KB2040 faces the rear USB-C opening, and the Keycapsss adapter occupies the front underside area.

Print both parts on their broad exterior faces using 0.20 mm layers, four perimeters, and 25–35% infill. PETG is preferred. Heat the inserts into the top-shell bosses from underneath and stop them flush. M2×6 screws are the nominal intended length; verify that your screws engage without bottoming out.

Test-fit the real adapter and KB2040 before final wire trimming. Keep all wiring flat in the center channel and verify that neither PCB nor any solder joint presses against the Cirque underside.
