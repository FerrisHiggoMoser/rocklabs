# Rounded, near-borderless Cirque enclosure

Size remains **47 × 50 × 13.7 mm**, with four M2 screws and M2×4 heat-set inserts.

## Appearance changes

- The top perimeter has a **1.8 mm continuous radius**, removing the sharp top edge.
- The Cirque sits in a **Ø40.5 × 0.50 mm flush recess**.
- The visible opening is **Ø38.8 mm**, leaving an approximately **0.85 mm retaining rim** around the 40.5 mm seat.
- The retaining rim is level with the recessed touch surface rather than forming a raised border.

The rim cannot be removed completely because something printed must stop the sensor from passing through the opening. At 0.85 mm it is near the practical FDM limit while still being printable with four perimeters and reasonably durable.

## Critical flush-fit check

The exact overlay/top-film height of the delivered Cirque revision determines perfect flushness. The model uses a conservative **0.50 mm recess**. First print a thin top-section test or measure the actual edge thickness with calipers. If necessary, adjust the recess by about ±0.15 mm in the STEP/source before the full print. A slightly shallow recess is safer than allowing the shell to press the touch surface.

Print the top with the broad upper face on the bed only if that surface quality is acceptable; otherwise print it open-side down and use a well-tuned top surface. PETG, 0.20 mm layers, four perimeters, and 25–35% infill are recommended.
