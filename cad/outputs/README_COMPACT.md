# Cirque TM040040 compact enclosure — revision 1

This is the smallest conservative version based on the purchased parts: **52 × 56 × 14.8 mm**, excluding the adhesive feet. It is 36% smaller in plan area than the original 62 × 70 mm enclosure.

The 40 mm Cirque, Keycapsss adapter, and KB2040 overlap in plan view and occupy separate vertical layers. The remaining width is set mainly by the Ø40.5 mm sensor pocket, 2.2 mm walls, and four M2 insert bosses. The depth is set by the sensor, rear USB-C plug access, and screw bosses.

## Key dimensions

- Body: **52 × 56 × 14.8 mm**
- Touch opening: **Ø37.8 mm**
- Cirque pocket: **Ø40.5 mm**, allowing for the conservative 5.61 mm assembled envelope
- USB-C opening: **10.2 × 4.6 mm**
- Bottom plate: **2.4 mm**
- Heat-set bores: **Ø3.2 mm**, intended for M2×4 inserts
- Foot recesses: **Ø10.4 × 0.35 mm**

## Print and test-fit

Print the two STLs with their broad exterior faces on the bed, using 0.20 mm layers, four perimeters, and 20–30% infill. PETG is preferred for the integrated sensor retainers. No supports should normally be needed.

This compact layout has much less service-loop room. Test-fit the actual Keycapsss adapter and KB2040 before soldering wires to final length. Route the FFC through the open rear side of the three-point Cirque retainer. Keep AWG30 wiring flat against the bottom and confirm that no component or solder joint is compressed when the plate closes.

Use four M2 screws long enough to pass through the 2.4 mm plate and engage the inserts; M2×6 is the intended nominal length. Confirm screw length against the parts you bought before tightening.

The adapter tray accepts approximately **28.5 × 19 mm**. Connector height and exact PCB outline can vary by kit revision, so compare the delivered board to this envelope before the full print.
