01 / ONE ATTACHMENT, BOTH POSITIONS

Set it down. Pick it up.

A slim padded lip fits beneath the case. The thick clamp block and its short screws sit outside the case, above the desk. Two permanent legs support the puck on rubber soles at the same height as the keyboard’s feet.

When you lift the keyboard, the case clamp and bolted retaining ring keep the puck captured. Its desk legs stay attached. No swapping parts or changing the angle. The existing enclosure, rubber feet, and rear USB access remain intact.

02 / FIT YOUR SETUP

A level keyboard starts with the feet.

The default needs 2 mm installed keyboard feet, an 8.2 mm closed case, 56 mm of straight edge, a bare 4 mm top ledge, and 8 mm of clear underside. The 1.2 mm lip and 0.5 mm pad leave a 0.3 mm gap above the desk. No keyboard risers are needed for that fit.

Print the fit check first and measure your actual feet. All keyboard feet and both puck soles must touch together without rocking. Shorter feet or thicker pads need a revised fit. Check key travel, case grip, and ordinary touch pressure before suspended use.
